---
name: Global visual direction
description: Visual-content constraint for Potential.com public-facing platform and marketing pages.
---

Use an international, diverse, enterprise-oriented visual language. Avoid Emirati or UAE-specific people, clothing, landmarks, flags, and cultural cues in general platform-positioning visuals.

**Why:** Potential.com is positioned as a globally relevant platform rather than a locally specific offering.

**How to apply:** For new or replacement marketing imagery, favor diverse professional audiences and international organisational contexts. UAE-specific material may remain where it is a factual part of a named client or case study.
