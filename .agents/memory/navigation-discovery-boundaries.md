---
name: Navigation and discovery boundaries
description: Durable rules for the public navigation, commercial CTA and optional Ayla discovery paths.
---

The public commercial journey should lead to `/inquire`; platform exploration should lead to `/platform`. Ayla is an optional discovery path and should remain limited to the homepage Meet Ayla section and the footer Talk to Ayla link. Obsolete pricing routes should redirect to `/inquire` and stay out of the sitemap.

**Why:** The platform is positioned for human-led programme consultation, while Ayla is a secondary discovery option rather than the primary site-wide conversion path.

**How to apply:** Preserve these boundaries when adding shared navigation, CTA buttons, SEO routes, redirects or new page sections. Do not change Ayla’s existing form, backend or voice-call behavior.
