---
name: Protect Ayla call flow
description: Constraint for any future edits to the Ayla page and its conversion experience.
---

Do not rebuild, simplify, replace, or otherwise alter the existing Ayla form, its fields, submission behavior, backend integration, or the immediate AI voice-call startup after a successful submission. Surrounding page copy and presentation may be refined without touching that flow.

**Why:** The immediate voice conversation is an intentional and required part of the conversion experience, not a standard lead form that can be safely replaced.

**How to apply:** For Ayla page work, isolate and preserve the complete form-to-call path. Do not add a second form, booking widget, qualification score, or competing conversion flow.
